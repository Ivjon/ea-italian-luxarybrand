Luxury Italian Brand

EA Italian Luxury Brand brings the great Italian fashion houses together under one roof in Tirana: Armani, Moschino, Versace, Valentino, Dolce & Gabbana and Philipp Plein, chosen piece by piece for their craft, their cut and their character.

We believe luxury should feel calm rather than loud: a coat you will wear for years, the right pair of shoes, a bag that only gets better with time. Our team will help you find the pieces that suit the way you live, in our boutique at Toptani Shopping Center or here online.
